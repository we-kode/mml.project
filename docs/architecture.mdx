## Federation

Instances can synchronize [records](./concepts/records) with partner instances. The Media services of the partners communicate directly over HTTPS through the reverse proxy; Identity is not involved. Requests between partners are signed with keys owned by the Media service, records are transferred in resumable parts and admins are informed over the SignalR hub `/hub/federation`. See [Federation](./concepts/federation) for details.
